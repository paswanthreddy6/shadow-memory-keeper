# SHADOW — 2–3 minute demo
1. Open Overview. Read the tagline.
2. Explain Retain -> Recall -> Reflect using the three cards.
3. Click **Load Demo Memory** and watch "Retaining memories... SHADOW is ready."
4. Open Customer Feedback: Acme Corp, Brightline, Lumen checkout complaints.
5. Open Product Decisions: "Postpone one-click checkout" with its rationale.
6. Ask SHADOW: "What did our team previously decide about checkout?"
7. Point to the Evidence panel — real Hindsight memories.
8. Ask: "Why was one-click checkout postponed?"
9. Show the link to the fraud / identity briefing.
10. Ask: "What patterns do you see across our checkout feedback?"
11. Show the synthesis across multiple customers and the competitor.
